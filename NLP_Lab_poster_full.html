Problem Statement & Motivation
Enterprise support ticketing systems receive thousands of unstructured text tickets daily. Manual routing and analysis is slow, error-prone, and does not scale. We build a GenAI-powered evaluation framework with three components: automatic classification of ticket type, queue and tags; entity extraction of key information such as locations, products and dates; and a human-in-the-loop annotation tool to create and refine gold-standard datasets. All components are evaluated on 1 000 examples and compared systematically across zero-shot, few-shot, and agent-based methods.

Provenance tracking: model-suggested vs human-added
